Village Guided Bike Tour

Bike Tours Diani Watamu

About This Tour

Ride through the heart of local village life on this guided bike tour through Diani and Watamu. Your local guide leads the way past homesteads, markets, and everyday coastal life you won't find in a guidebook.

Expect friendly greetings, real conversations, and a close-up look at how the community lives, works, and celebrates along the coast.

What's Included

Included

  • A local, English-speaking guide
  • Bike, helmet, and lock (or tuk tuk & driver, where applicable)
  • Bottled water

Not Included

  • Personal travel insurance
  • Tips and gratuities
  • Hotel pickup outside the Diani/Watamu core area

What To Carry

  • Sunscreen, hat, and sunglasses
  • Comfortable closed shoes
  • Camera or phone for photos
  • Cash for personal spending
