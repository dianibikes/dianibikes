Our Mission

Fun, Eco-Friendly, and Authentic

To inspire and empower adventurers of all ages and abilities to discover the wonders of the Coast in a fun, eco-friendly, and authentic way — on two wheels, where every pedal stroke brings you closer to nature, culture, and unforgettable memories.

"We believe the best way to experience a destination is on two wheels — where every pedal stroke brings you closer to nature, culture, and unforgettable memories."
— Masha Kirao, Founder & Team Leader
